Who it's for

A coaching resource built for youth climbing classes and opened up for other coaches. It has a September–June plan for seven age groups, with a plan for every week (vocabulary pitched to the age, plus a warm-up, skill drill, game and cool-down timed to the class length).

  • Head and assistant coaches running youth climbing classes, clubs and teams.
  • Coaches on the floor who need today's plan on a phone, or a printed week plan.
  • Program leads planning a full season from toddlers with a grown-up to competitive teens.

The age groups are Parent & Child (ages 2–3), Ages 3–4, Ages 4–6, Ages 6–9, Ages 9–12, Ages 12+ (training) and Ages 14+ (competitive). Classes run 60 minutes (Parent & Child, Ages 3–4, Ages 4–6), 90 minutes (Ages 6–9) and 120 minutes (Ages 9–12, Ages 12+ (training), Ages 14+ (competitive)). December and June are Review & Play months.

Always follow your own gym's rules, staffing ratios and belay policies.

Language: managing risk

Climbing is inherently dangerousNothing here makes it risk-free. Every plan and activity is written around managing and avoiding risk: checking systems, clearing fall zones, spotting, and choosing when to commit to a move.
