About

I'm a youth climbing coach, and I built this hub as my own coaching resource. Other coaches can borrow, adapt and improve it.

The age groups are Parent & Child (ages 2–3), Ages 3–4, Ages 4–6, Ages 6–9, Ages 9–12, Ages 12+ (training) and Ages 14+ (competitive). Classes run 60 minutes (Parent & Child, Ages 3–4, Ages 4–6), 90 minutes (Ages 6–9) and 120 minutes (Ages 9–12, Ages 12+ (training), Ages 14+ (competitive)). The curricula follow a month-by-month chart: four weeks a month, December and June as Find your Fun months, and one color per theme type.

Always follow your own gym's rules, staffing ratios and belay policies.
