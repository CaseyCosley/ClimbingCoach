About

I'm a youth climbing coach at Vertical World (Seattle and North), and I built this hub as my own coaching resource. It's my personal project, not an official Vertical World site, and it isn't endorsed by Vertical World.

The age groups match the youth classes I coach: Parent & Me (2–3), Rockin' Tots (3–4), Lil' Pebbles (4–6), Little Rockers (6–9), Base Club (9–12), Ascent Club (12+) and Summit Club (14+). Classes run 60 minutes (Parent & Me, Rockin' Tots, Lil' Pebbles), 90 minutes (Little Rockers) and 120 minutes (Base Club, Ascent, Summit). The curricula follow my month-by-month chart: four weeks a month, December and June as Find your Fun months, and one color per theme type.

It's shared so other coaches can borrow, adapt and improve it. Always follow your own gym's rules, staffing ratios and belay policies.
