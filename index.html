What this is

A coaching resource I built for my own youth climbing classes and opened up for other coaches. It has:

  • Curricula: a September–June chart for seven age groups, color coded by theme, with a plan for every week (vocabulary pitched to the age, plus a warm-up, skill drill, game and cool-down timed to the class length).
  • Activity Library: every game and drill with setup, steps, why it works, variations by age, how to manage its risks, and its source.
  • Vocabulary: climbing terms with a plain definition, a kid version, a little-kid version and a way to teach it.
  • Playbook: the research on how kids learn and what motivates them at each age, game-design ideas, and sources.

Who it's for

  • Head and assistant coaches running youth climbing classes, clubs and teams.
  • Coaches on the gym floor who need today's plan on a phone, or a printed week plan.
  • Program leads planning a full season from toddlers with a grown-up to competitive teens.
Climbing is inherently dangerousNothing here makes it risk-free. Every plan and activity is written around managing and avoiding risk: checking systems, clearing fall zones, spotting, and choosing when to commit to a move. Follow your own gym's rules and staffing ratios.
